About me

I started in customer relations and branch management, then spent more than five years in market research at Ipsos Nigeria, where I worked on consumer insights and field research.

That work taught me the part of analytics that tools cannot teach: starting with the business question and ending with a recommendation someone can act on.

I also run Nuve Accessories, a small business in Lagos, so I use data on my own sales as well as on client projects.
